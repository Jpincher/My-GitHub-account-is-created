About this course

An independent revision companion based on the curriculum linked by Montgomery County Public Schools, Maryland. It is not an official MCPS course.

Students normally take one maths course per year. These three pathways bring the combined Algebra 1, Geometry and Algebra 2 topic range into one place. Precalculus, AP, IB and magnet courses are outside this course.

Algebra 1 follows Amplify’s eight-topic course map. Geometry follows the eight-unit Illustrative Mathematics map. Algebra 2 follows the six MCPS overview PDFs, which carry 2015–2016 dates and remain linked by the district. Teacher-specific content and Honors depth may differ.
