Design

Turn “what if?” into “try this”.

Give the learner something to predict, investigate and explain. Then make the design decisions visible so the experience can be questioned and improved.

Design backwards from the outcome.

I connect the intended outcome to an activity, the support learners need and the evidence that will show understanding. I use ADDIE to structure the work while leaving room to revise decisions.

From “watch this” to “try this”.

The stick investigation moves from prediction to observation and explanation. The university pathway adds a model whose assumptions can be questioned. The learning task makes the reasoning visible, rather than hiding it behind a finished answer.

Build something people can challenge.

I use AI-supported prototyping to make ideas tangible. My role is to shape the learning, question the output and refine the experience. That builds on experience designing FEFC -accredited courses and delivering online and blended programmes through Skillkit.
